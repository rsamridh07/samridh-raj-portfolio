♙  About Me

Hi, I’m Samridh Raj 👋

I’m a B.Tech student pursuing Electrical and Electronics Engineering (EEE) at Bakhtiyarpur College of Engineering, with a strong passion for Robotics, Embedded Systems, Electronics and emerging technologies.

My journey into technology started with curiosity about how things work and a desire to turn creative ideas into practical innovations. From my JEE preparation days to entering the engineering world, every experience has taught me discipline, consistency and the importance of continuous learning.

I’m actively involved in robotics activities and have worked on projects like Line Following Robots using Arduino, IR sensors and motor drivers. As a team leader of Team Zentrix, I gained valuable experience in teamwork, project development and problem-solving.

I’ve also participated in college tech fests and explored the world of competitive robotics through the Micro Mouse event at IIT Patna – Celesta. Additionally, I participated in the Manak Carnival organized by the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS), Patna Branch, on the occasion of World Standards Day 2026.

Apart from hardware and robotics, I’m developing my programming skills in C and C++ and exploring Full-Stack Web Development to expand my technical capabilities.

My ultimate goal is to become a skilled Robotics and Embedded Systems Engineer and contribute to building intelligent autonomous machines and innovative technologies.

↓ Always Learning. Always Building. Always Improving.
